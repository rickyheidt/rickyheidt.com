## Snapshots

Newest run first. One block per competitor checked that run (template in PLAYBOOK.md section 9).

### Run of 2026-10-21 (run A, focus Carmel Valley and the 92130 corridor)

Method note: positions come from a web search API, not a live Google results page, so they are
approximate. rickyheidt.com did not appear for any of the 13 Carmel Valley queries.

#### SV Premier (group 1), svpremier.com
- Ranks for: "Carmel Valley housing market 2026", "92130 market report"; the main source cited in an
  AI style answer about Carmel Valley home prices.
- Sitemap: index of 5 child sitemaps, about 2,280 URLs (1,257 posts, 1,018 pages, 3 IDX), many are
  /es/ and /zh/ language copies. Child sitemaps modified 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-21. About 36 Carmel
  Valley URLs, including new construction subpages and a 2025 annual report. First snapshot, so no
  new URL diff yet.
- Winning pages: an annual Carmel Valley market report for 2025 (about 2,500 words plus a sold
  table, attached vs detached, buyer and seller takeaways, no FAQ or schema seen) and a Carmel Valley
  community page (quick facts, IDX search, video, community list, no Mello-Roos, HOA, fire or FAQ).
- Market report: annual only, latest covers 2025, MLS data.
- Decision: close now on freshness (monthly dated snapshot with a named source, done this run);
  backlog a deeper annual style 92130 report (price bands by product).

#### Amy Green, Coastal Premier Properties (group 1), amygreenrealtor.com
- Ranks for: "Carmel Valley real estate agent", through paid newswire releases (August 2026).
- Sitemap: index of 37 child sitemaps, about 265 content URLs plus IDX (33 static, 16 neighborhood,
  216 blog). About 37 Carmel Valley blog posts (schools, cost of living, commute, Carmel Valley vs
  Del Mar, housing market); several are about the Monterey County Carmel Valley.
- Winning page: /neighborhoods/carmel-valley, about 1,700 words, market stats dated "mid-2026"
  without a named source, commute section, FAQ (Mello-Roos and HOA, fire insurance, school zoning,
  Carmel Valley vs Del Mar, contingencies, escrow length), IDX listings.
- Market report: covers coastal areas, not Carmel Valley; looked stale.
- Decision: close now on the FAQ topics and official lookup links (done this run); backlog a
  Carmel Valley vs Del Mar comparison page and a commute guide.

#### Brokerage listing pages (group 2): Coldwell Banker, Douglas Elliman, RE/MAX, Century 21
- They rank for Mello-Roos and HOA queries only because individual listings expose those fields.
- Decision: ignore as competitors; answer the underlying question (how to look up a parcel's
  special taxes) on our page, done this run.

#### Portals (group 3): Redfin leads almost every query
- Decision: ignore the listing intent; target the informational and comparison versions.
