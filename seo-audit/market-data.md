### Inventory taken 2026-10-21

No page on the site quoted a market statistic (median price, sales count, days on market or inventory)
before this run. The only numbers found were general loan and cost rules of thumb on `index.html`,
`buy.html` and `faq.html` (3 percent and 3.5 percent minimum down payments, 2 to 3 percent closing
costs, a 1 percent Prop 13 base rate with 1.1 to 1.3 percent typical totals) and the listing and sales
facts on `16046-summer-sage-rd.html` and `sales.html`, which are protected. These are not market series,
so they are not tracked here. Source attributions note: the 10K Research PDFs name the source as the
"Greater San Diego Association of REALTORS®" (GSDAR) for SDAR ZIPs and "North San Diego County
REALTORS®" (NSDCAR) for the Carlsbad ZIPs.
